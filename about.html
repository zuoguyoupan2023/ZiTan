这是什么

字谈是一个帮助人们面对面沟通的免费、开源网页工具，主要为听障、语障及言语不便人士而做：说不方便的一方，把想说的话打成超大字号的消息卡片给对方看；对方直接说话，字谈会把语音实时转成文字显示在屏幕上。

它也不只属于无障碍人群——健常人之间的很多「开口不便」时刻都用得上（往下看场景清单）。不需要安装、不需要注册账号：手机或电脑浏览器打开 zitan.online 就能用，也可以添加到主屏幕当作 App 离线使用。

🟢 打字这一侧（听障 / 语障 / 不便开口的人）输入想说的话 → 大字卡片展示给对方看，字号可调，可开高对比度。
🔵 说话这一侧（志愿者 / 家人 / 店员…）对着手机正常说话 → 语音自动转成文字出现在对话里，还能把文字朗读出来。

谁可以用它

  • 听障人士：先天聋、突发性耳聋、老年性耳背——看大字，让对方说话转文字
  • 语障人士：失声、声音嘶哑、喉部术后、气管切开；渐冻症（ALS）、帕金森、脑瘫等导致的构音障碍；中风后失语；口吃严重期；选择性缄默——用打字代偿说话，需要时让 TTS 替你「读」出来
  • 暂时开不了口的人：拔牙 / 口腔咽喉手术后、住院插管恢复期

不止无障碍：人人能用的场景

  • 跨语言交流 🌍：出国旅行问路点餐、接待外国朋友——中英互译后用免费的朗读读给对方听；网页打开即用，不用装付费翻译 App
  • 学外语：把句子译成外语并朗读示范发音；或对方讲外语，你看实时字幕对照拼写
  • 保护嗓子：咽炎、教师 / 主播 / 歌手嗓音沙哑期——少说话，多打字
  • 安静场合：图书馆、电影院放映中、会议进行到一半、宝宝午睡、深夜宿舍、高铁静音车厢——无声传话不打扰
  • 隔着玻璃 / 有距离：银行柜台、医院取药窗口、车站售票亭、后厨 ↔ 前厅——隔着玻璃听不清，看大字一眼明白
  • 环境嘈杂：工厂车间、集市、酒吧音乐震天——听不清就把字亮出来
  • 口罩 / 面罩之下：医院、实验室、无尘车间，说话含糊就直接看字
  • 升级版纸条：点餐备注、问路、交换联系方式——比递纸笔快，常用句还能收藏一键重发

主要功能

  • 大字笔谈：超大字体消息卡片，字号无级调节，高对比度模式适合低视力用户
  • 语音转文字，三条路线按需选择：
    • 系统原生识别（Web Speech API）——联网即用、零下载
    • 设备端本地识别（SenseVoice）——离线可用，音频和文字都不离开设备
    • 云端识别（可选）——由你的服务配置决定
  • 朗读（TTS）：把你打的字读出来——替说不出话的人发声，也替跨语言的人翻译发音
  • 中英互译：跨语言场景直接聊
  • 快捷用语 / 收藏 / 对话历史 / 图片相册：高频句子一键发送，重要内容随时回看

隐私承诺

  • 本地识别路线：语音识别在你的设备上完成，音频和文字不上传
  • 不要求注册账号，没有广告，不做行为追踪
  • 若主动选择原生/云端识别路线，界面有明确标识，随时可切回本地模式

技术实现（写给感兴趣的开发者）

  • 单文件 HTML 应用 + Service Worker 的 PWA，核心功能纯前端即可运行
  • 设备端识别：SenseVoice-Small（int8 ONNX，约 228MB）跑在 onnxruntime-web（WASM）上；模型首次进入语音功能时才下载，分片存入 IndexedDB，之后离线可用
  • 代码 MIT 开源；第三方资源各自遵循其许可证（SenseVoice Apache-2.0、onnxruntime-web MIT 等）

常见问题

字谈是做什么的？
帮人们在面对面场景下快速沟通：一边打大字，一边说话自动转文字。为听障、语障人士而做，也服务于一切「开口不便」的时刻。
除了听障朋友，还有谁适合用？
所有说话不方便的人和时刻：语障人士（失声、喉部术后、渐冻症/帕金森等构音障碍、失语症、选择性缄默）、暂时开不了口（拔牙、术后、插管恢复期），以及嗓子哑了但还要办事的普通人。
听力和言语都正常的人能用它做什么？
当免费的跨语言沟通器（中英互译 + 朗读，旅行、接待外国朋友）；图书馆影院里无声传话；隔着银行/药房玻璃窗沟通；开会或宝宝睡着时递话；学外语做翻译和发音对照。
要装 App 吗？收费吗？
不装、不注册、不收费、无广告。浏览器打开即用，也可加到主屏幕离线用。
语音会被上传吗？
选本地识别就不会——全程在设备上完成。原生/云端路线为可选项，界面上有明确标识。

What is ZiTan?

ZiTan is a free, open-source web tool for face-to-face conversation, built primarily for deaf, hard-of-hearing and speech-impaired people: whoever can't easily speak types into extra-large text cards; the other person simply speaks, and their words are transcribed to text in real time on the same screen.

It's not only for accessibility — fully hearing, speaking people hit plenty of "can't just talk" moments too (see the scenario list below). No install, no sign-up: open zitan.online in any modern browser, or add it to your home screen and use it like an app, even offline.

🟢 Typing side (deaf / speech-impaired / can't talk now)Type what you want to say → shown as large, easy-to-read cards, with adjustable font size and a high-contrast mode.
🔵 Speaking side (volunteer / family / staff…)Just speak normally → speech is transcribed into the conversation automatically, and can be read aloud.

Who uses it

  • Deaf & hard-of-hearing people — including age-related hearing loss: read the large text while the other side speaks
  • Speech-impaired people: voice loss, hoarseness, laryngeal surgery, tracheostomy; motor-speech disorders from ALS, Parkinson's or cerebral palsy; post-stroke aphasia; severe stuttering; selective mutism — typing replaces speaking, with optional TTS reading your words aloud
  • People temporarily unable to speak: dental work, oral/throat surgery, intubation recovery

Beyond accessibility: everyday scenarios

  • Cross-language chat 🌍: asking directions or ordering food abroad, hosting foreign guests — translate Chinese↔English and let the free read-aloud speak for you. Opens in a browser; no paid translator app needed
  • Language learning: see your sentence translated and pronounced; or watch live captions of what the other person says in a foreign language
  • Saving your voice: laryngitis, teachers/streamers/singers on vocal rest — type more, talk less
  • Quiet places: libraries, mid-movie, mid-meeting, a napping baby, late-night dorms, quiet train cars — pass messages silently
  • Through glass / across distance: bank counters, pharmacy windows, ticket booths, kitchen ↔ front desk — when glass makes voices muffled, big text reads clearly
  • Noisy environments: workshops, markets, loud bars — when ears fail, show the words
  • Under masks / visors: hospitals, labs, clean rooms
  • A better paper note: order notes, directions, exchanging contacts — faster than pen and paper, with quick phrases you can save and reuse

Features

  • Large-text conversation: extra-large message cards, stepless font sizing, high-contrast mode
  • Real-time speech-to-text with three selectable routes:
    • Native Web Speech API — zero download, works online
    • Fully on-device recognition (SenseVoice) — works offline, audio and text never leave the device
    • Optional cloud ASR — configurable with your own service keys
  • Text-to-speech read-aloud — speaks for those who can't, and pronounces translations for cross-language chats
  • Chinese ↔ English translation
  • Quick phrases, saved sentences, history, photo sharing

Privacy

  • On-device route: recognition runs locally — audio and text are never uploaded
  • No accounts, no ads, no behavioral tracking
  • Native/cloud routes are opt-in, clearly labeled, and can be switched off anytime

Under the hood

  • Single-file HTML PWA + Service Worker; core features run entirely client-side
  • On-device ASR: SenseVoice-Small (int8 ONNX, ~228MB) running on onnxruntime-web (WASM); the model downloads lazily on first use and is cached shard-by-shard in IndexedDB for offline use
  • Code licensed MIT; third-party assets keep their own licenses (SenseVoice Apache-2.0, onnxruntime-web MIT, etc.)

FAQ

Who is ZiTan for?
Built for deaf, hard-of-hearing and speech-impaired people — and for anyone in a moment where talking doesn't work: noise, glass, quiet zones, a hoarse throat, or a different language.
Who else benefits besides deaf users?
People with speech impairments (voice loss, post-laryngectomy, ALS/Parkinson's-related dysarthria, aphasia, selective mutism), and people temporarily unable to speak after dental or oral surgery or during intubation recovery.
Two fully hearing, speaking people — why use it?
A free zh↔en translator with read-aloud for travel and guests; silent messaging in libraries and cinemas; talking through glass at counters; passing notes mid-meeting; translation practice while learning languages.
Is it free? Open source?
Free, no ads, MIT-licensed on GitHub.
Does my voice get uploaded?
Not on the local route — everything stays on the device. Native and cloud routes are optional and clearly labeled.
